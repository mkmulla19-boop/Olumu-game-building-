# Olomu Survival — Game Vision

**Title:** Olomu Survival  
**Genre:** 3D Third-person Survival / Adventure RPG  
**Platform:** Android first  
**Engine:** Godot 4.7.1  
**Visual Style:** Stylized low-poly 3D (beautiful but mobile-optimized)  
**Setting:** Olomu, Delta State, Nigeria (Urhobo community)

---

## Opening Sequence

The game begins in **Olomu**, a peaceful Urhobo community surrounded by thick vegetation, farms, rivers, dirt paths and smaller neighboring settlements.

The player is a young member of the community living with their family.

Life is normal at first:
- People farming, hunting, gathering food
- Trading and crafting tools
- Daily movement around the village

Then something changes.

A conflict begins between Olomu and a neighboring community.

The player doesn’t fully understand what is happening. There is confusion. People are running. Sounds of fighting can be heard in the distance.

The player’s father finds them and tells them to run.

He gives the player only what they can carry and says:

> “Go. Don’t look back. Survive, and find your way home.”

The player escapes into the surrounding wilderness.

**That is where the real game begins.**

---

## The Player’s Journey

The player must survive in the Nigerian wilderness while trying to find a safe place.

They begin with almost nothing.

Core activities:
- Gather plants and resources
- Collect wood and stone
- Hunt and obtain food
- Find clean water
- Build fires
- Create shelter
- Craft tools and equipment
- Manage inventory
- Encounter wildlife
- Defend against threats
- Discover other communities
- Meet NPCs and build relationships
- Uncover what really caused the conflict
- Explore the wider region
- Eventually establish their own settlement

**Ultimate Goal:**  
Find their family, understand what happened to Olomu, and return home.

---

## The World

The world starts small and expands naturally.

**Early areas:**
- Olomu village
- Surrounding forest
- Farms
- River
- Neighboring settlements

**Later expansion:**
- Larger villages
- Forests, rivers and swamps
- Trading settlements
- Roads
- Ancient locations
- Dangerous wilderness
- Larger communities

The world should feel alive:
- NPCs work, walk, trade, farm and interact
- Animals wander naturally
- Day / night cycle
- Weather (rain, storms) later

---

## Character

Protagonist: Young Urhobo character

Progression systems:
- Different hairstyles and clothing (traditional + modern)
- Weapons / tools / equipment
- Inventory
- Animation states
- Skills
- Health / Hunger / Thirst / Stamina
- Skill-tree progression

---

## Development Priority — Vertical Slice First

**Do not try to build the entire game at once.**

First create a real playable vertical slice:

**Small Olomu village containing:**
- Third-person player
- Touch controls + camera
- Walking / running
- Basic character animation
- Ground and environment
- Trees and vegetation
- Village buildings
- Simple NPCs
- Basic wildlife
- Gathering
- Inventory
- Hunger / Thirst
- Simple crafting
- Save / Load
- Opening story sequence (father’s order to run)

Everything must be designed **Android-first** (low polygon counts, efficient textures, reasonable draw calls).

Use free low-poly assets wherever possible. Reserve custom 3D generation only for things that make Olomu visually unique.

---

## Most Important Rule

> Do not make another throwaway prototype.  
> Build every system so it can remain in the final game.  
> The first village is not the game — it is the foundation of the game.
